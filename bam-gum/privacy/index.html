Bam Gum: Block Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Bam Gum: Block Puzzle (the “app”) is a casual block puzzle game published by Loshe Lab (Semih Basrık) on Google Play and the Apple App Store. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the app on either store.

Information we store on your device

  • Best score and progress preferences
  • Gold balance and purchase credit ledger (to prevent duplicate rewards)
  • Audio mute preference

This data stays on your device via normal app storage / preferences. We do not operate a Bam Gum account server.

Purchases

Optional gold packs are sold as consumable in-app products. Payment is processed by the store you use: Google Play Billing on Android, or Apple’s In-App Purchase on iOS. The store may share purchase tokens or transaction identifiers with the app so we can unlock gold. We do not receive your full card number.

Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. Apple’s practices are described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No ads SDK in the current build
  • No login / social graph
  • No sale of personal information

How to request deletion of your data

This section applies to Bam Gum: Block Puzzle, published by Loshe Lab (Semih Basrık).

Steps to request deletion:

  1. Email sbasrik@outlook.com with the subject line “Bam Gum data deletion request”.
  2. If your request relates to a purchase, include either your Google Play order ID (from your Google Play purchase email or Play order history) or your Apple transaction / order ID (from your App Store purchase email or Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → Purchase History).
  3. We will confirm by email. We aim to complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted:

  • Any Bam Gum-related information we hold in email support correspondence about your request (after we finish handling it, unless we must keep a short record of the request itself).
  • On-device game data (best score, gold balance, mute preference, purchase credit ledger) is removed when you uninstall the app from that device. We do not run a Bam Gum account server that stores this data for you.

What may be kept:

  • Purchase and payment records processed by Google Play remain under Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s retention rules. We cannot delete Google’s billing records for you.
  • Purchase and payment records processed by Apple remain under Apple’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s retention rules. We cannot delete Apple’s billing records for you.
  • We may retain limited records of deletion requests (for example, date and email) for a reasonable period to show we handled the request, then delete or anonymize them.

Fastest way to clear local game data on a device: uninstall Bam Gum: Block Puzzle.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, contact us at sbasrik@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
