Bam Gum: Block Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Bam Gum: Block Puzzle (the “app”) is a casual block puzzle game published by Loshe Lab (Semih Basrık) on Google Play. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the app.

Information we store on your device

  • Best score and progress preferences
  • Gold balance and purchase credit ledger (to prevent duplicate rewards)
  • Audio mute preference

This data stays on your device via normal app storage / preferences. We do not operate a Bam Gum account server.

Purchases

Optional gold packs are sold as Google Play consumable in-app products. Payment is processed by Google. Google may share purchase tokens with the app so we can unlock gold. We do not receive your full card number. Google’s own practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No ads SDK in the current build
  • No login / social graph
  • No sale of personal information

Deleting local data

To request removal of local app data associated with your use of the game, email the publisher at sbasrik@outlook.com. Uninstalling the app from your device also removes the local preferences stored by the app on that device.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, contact us at sbasrik@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
