Privacy Policy

Padel Coach (“we”, “the app”) helps players discover padel coaches, send coaching requests, and message coaches.

Data we process

  • Account info from Google Sign-In (name, email, profile photo, provider user id)
  • Profile settings you choose (role, timezone, currency, locale, notification preference)
  • Approximate location (optional) to rank nearby coaches — only when you grant permission
  • Messages, coaching requests, favourites, and ongoing coaching relationships you create in the app
  • Purchase / entitlement state for optional Google Play digital products (Player Pro, Coach Boost, packs)

How we use data

  • Authenticate you and keep your session
  • Provide search, messaging, requests, and profile features
  • Deliver paid digital features you buy through Google Play
  • Operate, secure, and improve the product

Sharing

  • We use Supabase to store app data under our control. We do not sell your personal data.
  • Google processes sign-in according to its policies when you use Sign in with Google.
  • Optional digital subscriptions and one-time packs are billed by Google Play. Google processes those payments under Google Play’s terms.
  • Coaching lesson fees are arranged directly between players and coaches and are not processed by Padel Coach or through this app.

Your choices

  • You can sign out anytime from Profile.
  • You can delete your account from Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile and related app data we control.
  • You can deny location permission; search still works without nearby ranking.
  • Manage or cancel Play subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Children

  • Padel Coach is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.
